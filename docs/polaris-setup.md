# Polaris · AstroNexus 2026 campus wayfinder

Upload **all files in this folder together** to your website (same folder, over https):
`polaris.html`, `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`.

Polaris has two parts: the **campus map** with turn-by-turn directions, and **today's timeline** (what is happening, when and where). Tap any event on the timeline to get directions to it.

## Kiosk screens (no internet needed on the day)
Put a tablet or laptop at key points. Each one shows "You are here" for its spot.

| Screen at | Open this link once (with internet) |
|---|---|
| School Reception | `https://astronexusnms.com/polaris.html?kiosk=recep` |
| Canteen (registration) | `https://astronexusnms.com/polaris.html?kiosk=canteen` |
| Zayed Hall foyer | `https://astronexusnms.com/polaris.html?kiosk=zayed` |
| Champions Club | `https://astronexusnms.com/polaris.html?kiosk=champ` |
| Exit 3 / Exit 4 | `https://astronexusnms.com/polaris.html?kiosk=exit3` / `?kiosk=exit4` |

1. **Before 5 Nov, with internet:** open the link in Chrome, wait for the map, then open it once more. Polaris is now saved on the device.
2. **On the day:** it works with **no Wi-Fi or data**. Touch the screen once to go full screen. After about 50 seconds without a touch it returns to the welcome screen.
3. Keep the device plugged in, auto-lock off, brightness high.

## Phones
Anyone who opens `polaris.html` once with internet keeps a working copy offline. Ask schools to open the link the day before. "Share > Add to Home Screen" adds a **Polaris** app icon.

## Notes
- Lost in Orbit is in the **Common Room, 3rd floor, directly above the Champions Club**. Polaris routes teams through the west wing and up the west wing stairs to the 3rd floor.
- The rocket launch on the welcome screen comes from the AstroNexus flyer. Pressing **Open campus map** (or an event) blasts it off before the map opens.
