LaunchX

Design your own rocket.

The Al Amal Space Club at GEMS New Millennium School is proud to present LaunchX ’26, AstroNexus’s own rocketry event. Participants design their own model rocket — launched live from the school’s football field during the event.

Students work with a randomly selected teammate on the design and presentation of their rocket, in the spirit of inter-school camaraderie and healthy competition. Teams get an hour and a half for design and construction, followed by a presentation of their project and teamwork.

Eligibility

Grade 9–10

Team Size

2, Random Pairs

Build Time

1.5 Hours

Objective

  • Learn the design, engineering, and aerodynamic principles of rocketry.
  • Use technical sketches and research to draft rocket plans.
  • Fly the rockets from the school’s football field.
  • Present projects and teamwork to a panel of judges.

Format

  1. Volunteer briefing & event initiation
  2. Research, construction, drafting
  3. Liftoff — move to football field & launch
  4. Final presentation to judges

Judging

  • Design — scientific principle & build quality
  • Teamwork, camaraderie & delegation
  • Team identity & motivation
